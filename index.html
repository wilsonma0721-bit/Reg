CUHK SEEM 2026–27 Notes: (1) Major GPA includes ENGG, ESTR, and SEEM courses at 2000+ level plus non-SEEM courses marked with #, excluding Faculty Package and Foundation courses. (2) ENGG1040 is required prior to ENGG1125 for JUPAS admittees without HKDSE M1/M2 (unless exempted via Mathematics Placement Test). (3) CILTHK Full Exemption is granted upon completing MKTG2010, SEEM2520, SEEM3620/ESTR3514, SEEM4750/ESTR4510 plus an FYP in transport/logistics. (4) Editable Credits: click any units badge marked ✎ — in the Course Bank or inside a term — to freely change that course's units. Edited courses are highlighted in amber; use "Reset to Default" in the edit dialog, or "Reset All Custom Unit Edits" in the Templates menu, to restore official values. (5) Edit Course Info: click a course title (or the ✎ button on a term card) to rename its code / title / units without deleting it. (6) Weekly Timetable: switch views in the top bar; click a block to edit or an empty slot to add a Mon–Fri session with venue. Overlaps are flagged in red. (7) Smart Paste: in the Add-Course dialog, paste CUSIS-style text to auto-fill code, units, title and detected class sessions. (8) Delete vs Unassign: the × on a term card (and "Remove Course from Term" in a session dialog) only unassigns the course — it returns to the Course Bank and its sessions in that term are cleared. Permanent deletion happens only via "Delete Course" in the course edit dialog; deleted official courses can be restored from the Templates menu. The Weekly Timetable always mirrors the selected term's scheduled courses.
